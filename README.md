### Main line: Tunnelling informatics

**[Tunnelling informatics](https://sectio-aurea-q.github.io/tunnelling-informatics.html)** is the data-driven study of hydrogen tunnelling in enzymes: every measured kinetic isotope effect with its source, what such data can and cannot prove about tunnelling, the published claims re-examined against the data, and predictions from enzyme structure tested in the laboratory. Not to be confused with information systems in tunnel construction or with network tunnelling.

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| **MEG-NEN** | Curated database of measured kinetic isotope effects of enzymes, every value with its source and read twice. In construction, not yet public. |
| **[Hackfort Equation](https://sectio-aurea-q.github.io/hackfort-equation.html)** | The quantum fraction η = 1 − 1/κ: the part of a rate that exists only because of tunnelling. |
| **[ELIAH](https://sectio-aurea-q.github.io/eliah.html)** | Eckart tunnelling coefficients, checked against 1197 certified values. |
| **[ZENO](https://sectio-aurea-q.github.io/zeno.html)** | Separates the tunnelling fraction of an enzyme reaction from its isotope effect. |
| **[MEG-APSU](https://sectio-aurea-q.github.io/meg-apsu.html)** | Classifies enzymes from their structure as quantum-critical or classical; honest accuracy and limits on its page. |

### Number theory: Radix Riemanniana

**[Radix Riemanniana](https://sectio-aurea-q.github.io/radix-riemanniana.html)**: the reproducing kernel of the Weil form, built from the primes alone. It chooses, for each elliptic curve, the optimal test function for bounding its analytic rank; every bound is certified with ball arithmetic. Code: [radix-rank](https://github.com/sectio-aurea-q/radix-rank) · Preprint: [doi:10.5281/zenodo.23299461](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23299461)

### Security research: post-quantum cryptography

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[VÄRN-60 (Anu)](https://github.com/sectio-aurea-q/varn-60)** | Timing side-channel tests for ML-KEM (FIPS 203) decapsulation in five libraries, with negative and positive controls in every run. Version 2.0.0, Rust, Apache-2.0. Statements made with earlier versions are withdrawn; see its README. |
| **PQ-GHOST** | Passive survey of 52 public TLS endpoints for post-quantum readiness: 16 of 52 offered hybrid post-quantum key exchange. Handshake observation only, no intrusion. |
| **Process memory (MEGALODON P2)** | Local study on Apple Silicon: key material and secrets left in a process's own memory after use, readable with root and with System Integrity Protection disabled. 71 findings in 8 of 16 applications, reported to the vendors. Local only, no remote attack. |
| **Kyber timing (MEGALODON P1)** | Withdrawn. The timing difference was an artefact of measuring one class after another. |

Findings are reported to the vendors before publication, and claims are kept to what the measurements support, including withdrawing a finding when a closer look does not hold up.
